Cold

Sophie holds the best reply rate (20.5%) on the smallest volume; Chloe runs the highest volume (63 leads) at the weakest rate among members with tracked data (9.5%) — review targeting quality before increasing send volume.

Warm

All four members land within a narrow band (20–23%) — nobody has broken away. Worth A/B-testing message content and report format per client industry to find the lever.

04
Findings & recommendations
What the numbers say, and what to do next.
Win rate

Steve (34.2%) and Sophie (33.3%) convert roughly half again as well as Chloe (21.6%) on comparable pipeline value. Chloe creates the most deals (51) but converts the fewest — the bottleneck is qualification, not volume.

Deal size

Steve carries the largest average deal (66,842 USD) and the highest won value; his pipeline skews to ODC and T&M work, the two highest-value contract types.

Capacity

Kai runs the smallest pipeline (29 deals) at a mid-pack win rate, and sits at — — the only member in the red band. There is headroom to raise volume without hurting conversion: reallocating a slice of Chloe's unqualified cold leads to Kai is the cheapest test.

Watch

Won value (3,030,000 USD) is 33.9% of total deal value. Deals that neither close nor drop out inflate the pipeline — enforce a stage-age limit so stale deals are marked lost rather than carried.
